Keep it on your phone

You add Settled to your home screen from your browser, so there's no app store and no big download. Once it's on your phone it works without signal too, so it's there when you need it.

iPhone and iPad
Open the app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
Android
Open the app in Chrome and tap Install. If it doesn't ask, it's under Install app in the menu.
Computer
In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right of the address bar.
